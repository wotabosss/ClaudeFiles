Read first
What's in the vial matters more than any date in this app. Research-grade material comes with no purity assay, no sterility certificate and no endotoxin testing. Underdosed and mislabelled content is common. Endotoxin in particular survives sterilisation, isn't affected by refrigeration, and isn't touched by a swab or a discard date — it causes fever and systemic inflammation. Every calculation here assumes the label was accurate.
Don't stack the melanocortins. MT-I, MT-II and PT-141 all act on the same receptor family, so running them together multiplies effects rather than adding them. MT-II with PT-141 compounds both the priapism and the blood-pressure risk. An erection lasting over 4 hours is a medical emergency — go to an emergency department, don't wait it out.
Mole mapping before either melanotan. Both darken existing naevi and can obscure the early signs of melanoma — the change you'd want to catch gets hidden by the thing causing it. Australia has among the highest melanoma rates in the world, so get a baseline skin check and mapping first, then regular reviews. This is the item on this list most likely to matter long-term, and it won't announce itself.
Retatrutide is the highest-risk item here. A GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon triple agonist is a significant systemic drug, and self-titrating one without bloodwork is the single thing worth changing ahead of everything else. It needs a prescriber and monitoring, not a schedule in an app.
Most of these are Schedule 4 in Australia — meaning prescribable. A GP or men's health clinic can put retatrutide on a supervised footing with real bloodwork, and a skin check with mole mapping is worth booking regardless of everything else. That's worth more than any tracking this app can do.
Every dose
Sterile technique every time: swab the stopper and the site and let it dry, new needle, rotate sites. Refrigerate after mixing at 2–8 °C.
Report anything unexpected to a doctor — fever after injecting, a hot or swollen injection site, chest symptoms, or persistent vomiting all warrant same-day care rather than waiting.

Active vials
Dates assume sterile technique and constant 2–8 °C, and are the conservative preservative standard — not a validated assay for these compounds. Keep vials dark. There is no reliable way to convert time left at room temperature into days of shelf life lost, and no way to see lost potency: if a vial has had a warm excursion you can't quantify, replacing it is the only sound call. Discard on cloudiness, floaters or discolouration.
